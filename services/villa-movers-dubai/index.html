Villa Movers in Dubai

Villas and townhouses moved with a crew sized for the volume — including the garden, the store room and the things nobody remembers until moving day.

A villa move is a volume problem. Three to five bedrooms, a maid's room, a store room, a garden with outdoor furniture and a barbecue, sometimes a gym or a piano, and a garage full of things that were put there years ago. Send too small a crew and the job runs into the night.

We size villa moves properly: enough packers to clear the kitchen and bedrooms in parallel, enough men to carry, and a truck that means one trip instead of three. We move villas and townhouses across Dubai, including gated communities like Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills, The Springs, Al Barsha and the Palm.

Included

What a villa move covers

  • Every room, including the ones you forget

    Store rooms, maid's rooms, garages, balconies and the cupboard under the stairs.

  • Garden and outdoor items

    Outdoor sofas, dining sets, parasols, barbecues and plant pots, wrapped and loaded last so they come off first.

  • Heavy and awkward pieces

    Wardrobes, American-size fridges, safes, gym equipment and pianos, handled with the right number of people and straps.

  • Full packing

    Kitchens, glassware, artwork and children's rooms, packed room by room and labelled so unpacking makes sense.

  • Dismantling and reassembly

    Beds, wardrobes, dining tables, desks and outdoor furniture taken apart and rebuilt at the new villa.

  • Community access

    Gate passes, security registration and parking arranged with you in advance where the community requires it.

Gated communities and access

Villa communities have their own rules rather than lift slots. Emaar communities such as Arabian Ranches and Dubai Hills, Nakheel communities on the Palm and in Jumeirah Islands, and most managed compounds want the moving company registered at the gate, sometimes with a permit or an NOC, and often with restrictions on which hours trucks may enter.

The Palm is its own case: one access road, limited turning space on the fronds and shoreline apartments with their own tower rules. We plan Palm moves around traffic rather than pretending it is not there.

Tell us the community when you ask for a price and we will tell you what the gate usually asks for.

Villa moving costs

Villas are priced on volume, bedrooms, stairs and access, plus how much packing you want. A four-bedroom villa with full packing needs a bigger crew and a longer day than the same villa where the family has packed its own boxes over the previous week.

Two things reduce cost more than anything else: packing your own non-fragile items, and moving mid-week rather than at a month-end weekend. Two things increase it: stairs, and specialty items that need extra people.

Villa moving questions

How many men and trucks does a villa move need?

It depends on the bedrooms and the volume. We confirm the crew and truck size with your quote, so you know what is turning up rather than hoping.

Can you move a villa in one day?

Most three and four-bedroom villas are done in a day when packing is finished beforehand. Full packing plus moving a five-bedroom villa is usually spread over two days.

Do you move garden furniture and plants?

Yes, outdoor furniture, barbecues and pots are all part of the move. Large plants travel best last-on, first-off, and we will tell you if something is too fragile to survive the trip.

Can you take a piano or a safe?

Yes, with the right crew and equipment. Mention it when you ask for a price, because it changes the plan for the day.

Can you store our furniture if the new villa is not ready?

Yes, through partner storage — we move it in, keep it, and deliver it when you are ready. See storage services.
