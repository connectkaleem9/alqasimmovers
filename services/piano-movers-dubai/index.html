Piano & Heavy Item Movers in Dubai

Pianos, safes, gym equipment and large appliances — moved by enough people, with the right straps and protection.

Heavy items are not moved differently because they are precious. They are moved differently because the weight is concentrated, the balance is awkward, and the consequences of a slip are serious for the item, the building and the people carrying it.

We move pianos, safes, gym equipment and large appliances across Dubai, as part of a move or on their own. The job is planned before the day: we ask about the weight, the route, the doorways, the stairs or lift, and the floor surface at both ends.

What we handle

Heavy items we move

  • Upright and digital pianos

    Wrapped, strapped and carried by a crew sized for the weight, with the route measured first.

  • Safes

    Heavy for their size, which is exactly why they need equipment rather than extra effort.

  • Gym equipment

    Treadmills, multi-gyms and weight stacks, dismantled where the manufacturer allows.

  • Large appliances

    American-style fridges, range cookers and washing machines, moved upright and secured.

  • Marble and glass tops

    Carried on edge with rigid protection, never flat.

  • Stairs and tight access

    Extra crew instead of shortcuts when there is no lift or the corner is tight.

Why we ask questions before quoting

For a heavy item, the price and the plan come from the access, not just the item. A piano on the ground floor next to a wide door is a different job from the same piano on the third floor of a building with a small lift.

So we will ask for the make and rough weight, the floor at both ends, the lift dimensions or number of stairs, doorway widths, and whether there is a straight route from the door to the parking. Photos or a short video answer most of it in seconds.

Grand pianos and specialist items

Grand pianos, antique instruments and very heavy safes need equipment and experience beyond a standard moving crew. Tell us exactly what you have and we will tell you honestly whether it is a job for us or for a specialist — that is a better outcome for everyone than finding out on the stairs. Heavy item questions

Can you move a piano up or down stairs?

Often yes, with a bigger crew and the right straps. It depends on the weight, the staircase and the turns, which is why we ask for details and photos first.

Do you move safes?

Yes. Tell us the weight and both floors, since access decides the crew and equipment.

Can you move gym equipment?

Yes, including treadmills and multi-gyms. Some machines must be partly dismantled, and we do that where the design allows.

Is a heavy item included in a normal house move?

Tell us about it when you ask for the price. It changes the crew size for the day, so it should be priced in, not sprung on the team on the morning.

Do you move items into villas with marble floors?

Yes, with floor protection along the route. Mention the flooring and we will bring what is needed.
