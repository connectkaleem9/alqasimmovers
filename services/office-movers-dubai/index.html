Office Movers in Dubai

Office relocation planned around your working hours, so the business loses a weekend rather than a week.

An office move is judged on one thing: whether people can work on Monday morning. That means desks rebuilt, chairs at the right desks, monitors and cables where they belong, and files in an order somebody can actually use.

We move offices across Dubai — Business Bay, JLT, Downtown, Deira, Al Quoz and the free-zone business parks — usually in the evening or over a weekend so that nobody loses a working day. Desks and workstations are dismantled, screens and IT equipment are wrapped, and everything is labelled by desk or department so it lands in the right place.

Included

What the office move includes

  • Out-of-hours moving

    Evenings and weekends wherever the buildings allow it, so the business keeps running.

  • Workstations and desks

    Dismantled, moved and rebuilt, with fittings bagged per desk.

  • IT equipment

    Monitors, PCs, printers and servers wrapped and boxed individually. We move and reconnect nothing electrical we are not asked to — your IT team keeps control of the network.

  • Files and archives

    Packed in order, labelled by department, and stacked so the cabinets can be refilled quickly.

  • Labelling by desk

    Boxes and furniture tagged to the new floor plan, so unloading follows your seating plan.

  • Both buildings handled

    Permits, lifts and loading bays arranged at the old and the new address.

Planning an office relocation

Start with a floor plan of the new office and a decision about who sits where. Almost every problem on moving day comes back to that one document. Once it exists, labelling is simple and the crew can unload straight into position.

Then the buildings: both towers will want permits and lift bookings, and commercial towers often restrict moves to evenings or weekends. Free-zone buildings may also need the moving company registered with security in advance.

Finally, decide what is not moving. Old chairs, broken desks and archive boxes nobody has opened in years cost the same to move as good furniture. Offices that clear out first always move faster.

Shops, restaurants and warehouses

Retail and warehouse moves are a different job from a desk-based office: stock, shelving, display units, fridges and equipment, usually with a hard deadline tied to a lease or a shopfit. We handle those too — see commercial moving.

Office moving questions

Can you move our office at the weekend?

Yes, and it is what we usually recommend. Most Dubai commercial towers restrict moving to outside business hours anyway.

Do you dismantle and rebuild workstations?

Yes. Desks, partitions and storage units are dismantled, moved and reassembled, with each desk's screws and fittings kept together.

Who handles the computers?

We wrap and transport them safely. Disconnecting and reconnecting the network, servers and phones stays with your IT provider, which is the safer arrangement for everyone.

Can you move us in stages?

Yes. Larger offices often move department by department over several evenings, so no team loses a full working day.

Do you provide boxes and crates for files?

Yes, boxes and packing materials come with the job, and files are packed in order rather than tipped in.
