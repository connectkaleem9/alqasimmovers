Apartment Movers in Dubai

Studio to three-bedroom flats, moved around the permit and the service-lift slot your building gives you.

A Dubai apartment move is decided by the building, not by the furniture. The tower gives you a permit and a two or three-hour service-lift slot, the loading bay is shared with deliveries, and the security desk will not let a crew upstairs without paperwork. A crew that has done it before works to that clock.

We move studios, one, two and three-bedroom apartments across every tower community in Dubai: Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay, Downtown, JVC, Al Barsha and the rest. Packing, dismantling, moving and reassembly are all part of the job.

Tower moves

Built around tower rules

  • Permit paperwork

  • Service-lift slots

    We plan the crew size so the load fits the hours the building gives you.

Move-in and move-out permits

Nearly every managed tower in Dubai requires a move permit. The details differ: Emaar-managed buildings in Downtown, DMCC in JLT and the Marina managements each have their own form and their own notice period, and many ask for a refundable deposit, a tenancy contract or an NOC from the landlord.

Two things are worth knowing. First, apply as soon as the date is fixed — the permit, not the truck, is what delays most moves. Second, moving hours are often restricted, especially at weekends, so the slot you are given decides the start time.

Send us your slot and we plan around it. If the building only allows an evening window, we work evenings.

Which apartment sizes we move

A studio or one-bedroom flat is usually a half-day job: a small crew, a small truck, one lift slot. Two and three-bedroom apartments take longer, mostly because of the kitchen and the wardrobes, and because more furniture has to come apart to get through the doors and into the lift.

If you are only moving a few pieces rather than a whole flat, the furniture moving service is usually the cheaper option. If you want everything packed for you, add packing and unpacking.

Apartment moving questions

How long does an apartment move take?

A studio or one-bedroom flat is typically a half day. A two-bedroom is most of a day, and a three-bedroom with full packing is a full day. Lift slots and waiting times at the loading bay are what change these numbers.

Do you arrange the move permit?

The permit is issued to the resident by building management, so you apply and we provide whatever crew or vehicle details they ask for. We will tell you what is usually needed so nothing is missed.

Can you move in the evening or at the weekend?

Yes. Many towers only allow moves at set hours, and evening slots are common. Tell us your window.

My building has no service lift. Can you still move me?

Yes. Older low-rise buildings in areas like Deira and Bur Dubai often have stairs only. We bring a bigger crew, since carrying takes longer than lifting.

Do you move single items into an apartment?

Yes — a bed, a sofa or a fridge on its own is a small job with a pickup and two men. See furniture movers.
