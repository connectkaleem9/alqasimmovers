Moving Services in Dubai

Packing, dismantling, moving, reassembly. Choose the service that matches your move, or call us and we will tell you what you actually need.

Al Qasim Movers is a Dubai moving company offering the full movers and packers service: we pack your belongings, dismantle the furniture, carry everything out, drive it to the new address, and put it back together. We work across all of Dubai and move to every emirate in the UAE.

Most people need one of the four household services below. If your move does not fit neatly into one of them, that is normal. Describe it to us and we will tell you which crew and truck size it needs.

Our Services

What we move

Every service includes the packing, the lifting and the reassembly, unless you ask us to do less.

  • Home movers

    Whole-house moves anywhere in Dubai, from one-bedroom flats to family homes.

  • Apartment movers

    Tower moves: building permits, service-lift booking and loading-bay timing.

  • Villa movers

    Large volumes, gated communities, garden and outdoor items.

  • Office movers

    Desks, chairs, IT and files moved outside working hours where possible.

  • Packing and unpacking

    Boxes, bubble wrap, blankets and stretch film, with fragile items wrapped individually.

  • Furniture movers

    A few pieces or a single item: a bed, a wardrobe, a sofa, a fridge.

What a move with us looks like

You send us a list, a few photos or a short video of what needs to move, along with both addresses and the floor. We give you a price. On the day, the crew arrives with tools, boxes and blankets, protects the floors and lifts, dismantles the beds and wardrobes, wraps everything, loads it, and unloads and reassembles at the other end.

We do not guess prices without knowing the volume, the floor and the access. A one-bedroom apartment on the ground floor with lift access is a different job from a four-bedroom villa with stairs.

Also available

  • Storage between moves

    If your new place is not ready, we can move your furniture into partner storage and deliver it later.

  • Same-day and urgent moves

    Sometimes possible, depending on crew and truck availability. Call to check — we never promise a same-day slot we cannot keep.

  • Moves to other emirates

    Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain and Al Ain.

  • Handyman work after the move

    Curtain and blind installation, TV wall mounting, and fixing wall-mounted furniture.

Common questions

Which areas of Dubai do you cover?

All of Dubai — Marina, JVC, Business Bay, Downtown, Palm Jumeirah, JLT, Al Barsha, Deira, Bur Dubai, Al Quoz, Silicon Oasis and everywhere between. See the areas we serve.

Do you move outside Dubai?

Yes, to every emirate in the UAE. We do not do international moves.

How much will my move cost?

It depends on the volume, the floors and the access at both ends. Send us a list or a video and you get a real number, not a range. Tell us what you are moving and we will send a price. Call 0556869224 or message us on WhatsApp.

How far in advance should I book?

Weekends and month-ends are the busiest, so a few days ahead is safer. Mid-week and mid-month moves are usually easier to slot in.
