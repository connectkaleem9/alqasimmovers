Commercial Movers in Dubai

Shops, restaurants and warehouses moved around trading hours, with stock and fixtures handled in an order that lets you reopen fast.

A commercial move is not an office move with more boxes. A shop has stock that has to stay sellable, display units that are awkward but not heavy, and a landlord or mall management with strict hours for deliveries and moves. A restaurant has equipment, cold storage and a fit-out schedule. A warehouse has racking, pallets and volume.

We move retail units, restaurants and cafés, and warehouse stock across Dubai, including mall units, showrooms in Al Quoz and Deira, and facilities in the industrial areas. The plan is built around your trading hours and your reopening date.

Included

What we move for businesses

  • Stock and inventory

    Boxed, labelled and loaded so it can be counted and shelved again quickly.

  • Shelving and racking

    Dismantled, moved and rebuilt at the new unit.

  • Display units and counters

    Wrapped and carried by enough people to keep the glass and finishes intact.

  • Restaurant equipment

    Fridges, prep tables and appliances moved upright and secured; connections stay with your technicians.

  • Overnight and off-hours moves

    Mall units and busy streets are easier at night, and many landlords insist on it.

  • Phased moves

    Move in stages so the business keeps trading while the new unit fills up.

Mall units, landlords and access

Malls and managed commercial buildings in Dubai usually require a work permit or a move approval, a fixed loading dock slot, and access through service corridors rather than the shop front. Some only allow work after closing.

Get the approval moving as soon as you have keys, and send us the slot. We would rather plan a night move properly than arrive at a dock that is booked until midnight.

Offices and small loads

If you are moving desks and staff rather than stock, the office movers page covers that. For a few boxes or one display unit between branches, a truck with a driver and loading crew is usually enough. Tell us what you are moving and we will send a price. Call 0556869224 or message us on WhatsApp.

Commercial moving questions

Can you move our shop overnight?

Yes. Night and off-hours moves are normal for retail, and many mall managements require them.

Do you move stock as well as fixtures?

Yes, both. Stock is boxed and labelled so it can be counted and put back on shelves quickly.

Can you handle racking and heavy shelving?

Yes, dismantled and rebuilt. Tell us the height and type so we bring the right crew.

Can you move a restaurant kitchen?

We move the equipment and furniture. Disconnecting and reconnecting gas, water and electrics stays with licensed technicians.

Can the move be done in phases?

Yes. Many businesses move stock first and fixtures last, or move department by department, so trading never fully stops.
