Furniture Dismantling & Assembly in Dubai

Beds, wardrobes and flat-pack furniture taken apart or put together — plus curtains, blinds and TVs once the furniture is in place.

Dismantling and assembly is the part of a move that takes the longest and goes wrong the most. A wardrobe forced out of a room in one piece damages the door frame. A bed rebuilt with missing screws is a bed that squeaks for a year. Flat-pack units are the worst of both: cheap to buy, slow to build, and easy to assemble in the wrong order.

We do this work as part of a move and as a job on its own. If you have bought new furniture and just need it built, or you need the old wardrobe taken apart before the new one arrives, that is a normal booking for us.

Included

What we take apart and put together

  • Beds and bunk beds

    Frames, slats and headboards, with fittings bagged and taped to the frame so nothing is lost.

  • Wardrobes and cabinets

    Dismantled when they will not fit through the door, then rebuilt and levelled in the new room.

  • Flat-pack furniture

    New units built from the box, packaging taken away afterwards.

  • Dining tables and desks

    Legs and extensions removed for transport and reattached at the other end.

  • Curtains and blinds

    Rails and tracks fitted and curtains hung once the furniture is in position.

  • TV wall mounting

    Brackets fitted and the screen mounted, with cables tidied behind it.

Handyman work after a move

The day after a move is when the small jobs appear: curtains to hang, a TV to mount, mirrors and shelves to fix to the wall, and furniture that needs securing so it cannot tip. We can do these at the end of the move rather than leaving you to find someone else.

Tell us what you need when you book, because brackets and fixings depend on the wall type, and we would rather bring the right ones than promise and improvise.

Booking assembly on its own

Assembly-only jobs are priced by the number and type of items, not by the hour — a bed and two wardrobes is a predictable job, and you should know the price before we arrive.

If the furniture also needs moving between addresses, look at furniture moving instead, since dismantling and reassembly are already part of it. Tell us what you are moving and we will send a price. Call 0556869224 or message us on WhatsApp.

Assembly questions

Can you assemble furniture I bought from IKEA or online?

Yes. New flat-pack furniture is one of the most common requests, and we take the packaging away when we finish.

Do you bring tools and fixings?

We bring the tools. Wall fixings depend on the wall, so tell us what you are mounting and where when you book.

Can you dismantle furniture I am selling or storing?

Yes, and we bag and label the fittings so whoever rebuilds it has everything.

Do you hang curtains and mount TVs?

Yes, curtain and blind installation, TV wall mounting, and fixing wall-mounted furniture.

Is assembly included when you move me?

Yes. In a home, apartment, villa or office move, taking furniture apart and rebuilding it is part of the service.
