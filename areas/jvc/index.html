Movers in JVC (Jumeirah Village Circle)

Hundreds of separate buildings with separate managers, plus townhouses — so no two JVC moves follow the same process.

JVC is a community of many small landlords and managements rather than a few large ones. One building may ask for a formal permit and a deposit, while the building opposite only wants a call to the watchman. That inconsistency is the main thing to plan for.

The other is the street layout. JVC's circular districts have narrow internal roads, buildings without proper loading bays, and on-street parking that fills up in the evening. Getting the truck close to the door is half the job.

Local detail

What a JVC move involves

  • Management varies by building

    We ask which building you are in, because the process genuinely differs from one to the next.

  • Small or no loading bays

    Many buildings load from the street, so timing and vehicle size matter.

  • Narrow internal roads

    District roads are tight; a smaller vehicle making two trips sometimes beats one large truck that cannot park.

  • Townhouses as well as flats

    Stairs instead of lifts, and more volume than a two-bedroom apartment.

  • Newer buildings, working lifts

    Most JVC stock is recent, so service lifts are usually available and in good order.

  • Popular for upsizing

    A lot of JVC moves are people moving from a studio or one-bed into something larger within the same community.

Apartments, townhouses and villas

For flats, the apartment moving service applies, including permits where the building asks for them. For townhouses and villas in JVC and the neighbouring JVT, villa moving is the closer fit — more volume, stairs and often a small garden.

Moving one room or a few pieces within JVC is common enough that furniture moving is often the cheaper booking. Send us the two addresses, the floor and what you are moving. Call 0556869224 or message us on WhatsApp.

Moving in JVC

Does my JVC building need a move permit?

Some do and some do not. Ask your building management or watchman a few days before, and tell us what they say.

Can a big truck get to my building?

Usually, but the internal roads are narrow. Tell us the district and building and we will choose the vehicle accordingly.

Do you move townhouses in JVC?

Yes. Townhouse moves are villa-style jobs: stairs, more volume and often outdoor items.

Can you move within JVC on the same day?

Short moves inside the community are often possible the same or next day, depending on availability.
