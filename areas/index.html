Areas We Serve in Dubai

All of Dubai, and moves to every emirate in the UAE. Below are the communities where the move has its own rules worth knowing about.

We move anywhere in Dubai. Not "selected areas", not "mainly Marina and Downtown" — everywhere, from the towers on Sheikh Zayed Road to the villa communities on the edges of the city, and from older buildings in Deira and Bur Dubai to new handovers in Dubai Hills.

What changes between areas is not whether we come, but how the move is planned. A tower gives you a permit and a two-hour lift slot. A gated villa community registers the truck at the gate. An older walk-up building needs a bigger crew because there is no service lift at all.

The pages below cover the communities where those details matter most. If your area is not listed, it is still served — call and we will tell you exactly how a move there usually works.
