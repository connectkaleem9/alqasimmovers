Terms & Conditions

How quotes, bookings and moves work, in plain language.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

These terms cover the use of alqasimmovers.com and services booked with Al Qasim Movers L.L.C, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The specific details of your move — price, date, crew, and payment terms — are what we confirm with you in writing when you book, and those confirmed details take priority over anything general on this page.

Quotes

Quotes are free and are based on the information you give us: property size, volume, floors, lift and access at both ends, and the packing you want. If the job turns out to be materially different from what was described — for example significantly more furniture, no working lift, or no vehicle access — the price and time may need to change, and we will tell you before we continue, not afterwards.

A quote is not a booking. Your date is reserved when we confirm it with you.

Your part

Please arrange any move permit, lift booking, gate pass or parking your building requires, and tell us the approved time window. Tell us in advance about heavy or fragile items, items that must not be moved, and anything that needs special handling.

Keep cash, jewellery, passports, documents and medicines with you rather than in the truck. Please make sure someone who can make decisions is reachable on moving day.

What we do not move

Hazardous or perishable goods: gas cylinders, flammable liquids, chemicals, explosives, open or fresh food, and anything illegal. We also do not carry out international moves, or disconnect and reconnect gas, water, electrical or network systems — those stay with licensed technicians or your IT provider.

If something falls outside what we can safely handle, we will tell you before the day rather than leave it on the truck ramp.

Changes, delays and cancellation

Tell us as early as you can if your date or details change, and we will do our best to re-book. Some things are outside anyone's control — building management refusing access, a lift out of service, road closures or weather. Where that happens we will agree the next step with you.

If something goes wrong

Tell us immediately, on the day if possible, and send photos. We will look into what happened and come back to you. Reporting straight away matters, because it lets us establish what happened while the crew and the load are still available.

Using this website

The content is kept accurate and current as far as we can; general information about moving in Dubai — permits, building rules, timings — is exactly that, general, because processes differ by building and change over time. Always check with your own building management.
