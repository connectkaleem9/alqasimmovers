Checklist

What we need to price it properly

The more of this you can tell us, the more accurate the number.

  • Property size

    Studio, one, two or three-bedroom apartment, villa, townhouse or office.

  • Both addresses

    Area and building, plus the floor and whether there is a lift at each end.

  • Your date

    And whether it is flexible. Mid-week and mid-month moves are easier to schedule.

  • What packing you want

    Everything, only the kitchen and fragile items, or nothing at all.

  • Heavy or special items

    Pianos, safes, gym equipment, large fridges, marble or glass tops.

  • Photos or a video

    A one-minute walkthrough answers more questions than any form can.

What happens next

  1. 01

    You send the details

    By form, WhatsApp or a call — whichever is easiest.

  2. 02

    We ask what is missing

    Usually the floor, the lift and the access. It takes a minute.

  3. 03

    You get a price

    A real number for your move, with the crew and truck size confirmed.

  4. 04

    You book a date

    We confirm the slot and tell you what to arrange with your building.

Quote questions

Is the quote really free?

Yes, and there is no obligation.

Why do you not publish fixed prices?

Because an honest price needs the volume, the floors and the access. A published price list either overcharges the easy moves or collapses on the hard ones.

Can the price change on moving day?

Only if the job is not what was described — for example, double the furniture or a lift that does not work. Tell us the real situation and the price stands.

Do you need to visit my home?

Usually not. Photos or a walkthrough video are enough for most moves. For large villas or offices, a visit is sometimes worth it.

How far ahead should I book?

A few days for a normal move. Month-end weekends fill up first, so book earlier for those.
