Before you call

What should I have ready?

Both addresses with floor numbers, the property size, your preferred date, and a note of anything heavy, fragile or unusual.

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes are free, and we would rather ask questions than give you a number that changes on moving day.

How quickly do you reply?

During opening hours, usually straight away on WhatsApp. Messages that arrive late at night are answered the next morning.

Do you have an office I can visit?

We are a service-based business working across Dubai, so the quickest route is a call, a WhatsApp message or the form above.
