Cookie Policy

What this website stores in your browser, and how you control it.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Analytics cookies (only if you accept)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site: which pages are visited, and whether people tap the call or WhatsApp buttons or send a quote request. This helps us improve the site. Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_<ID>, which are kept for up to 2 years.

These cookies are not set when you first open the site. Google Analytics is only loaded after you choose Accept in the cookie notice. If you choose Decline, it is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

Google processes this data as described in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your saved choice

To remember whether you accepted or declined, the site saves one small entry called aq-consent in your browser's local storage. It contains only the word "granted" or "denied" and is never sent to us.

Change your choice

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to change your choice at any time. If you withdraw consent, the analytics cookies are removed from your browser. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
