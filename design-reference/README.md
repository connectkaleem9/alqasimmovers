# Design Reference — put your design here

Drop whatever you have into this folder. I will read it and rebuild the site's design to match.

## What to put here

| What you have | What to do | Best for me |
|---|---|---|
| **Screenshots / images** of the design | Save them here as `.png` or `.jpg` | Name them by page: `home-desktop.png`, `home-mobile.png`, `services.png` |
| **A PDF** of the design | Save it here | Fine as-is |
| **Figma / Adobe XD / Canva link** | Just paste the link in the chat | Make sure the link is set to "anyone with the link can view" |
| **An HTML/CSS template** (bought or downloaded) | Put the whole folder here, e.g. `design-reference/template/` | I can read the code and match it exactly |
| **A website you like** | Paste the address in the chat | I can open it and copy the layout style (not its text or images) |
| **A PSD / Sketch / .fig file** | Save it here, but **also add a screenshot** | I cannot open those file formats — the screenshot is what I work from |

## Helpful, not required
- Both **desktop and mobile** views, if you have them.
- The **Arabic** version, if your design has one. If not, I will mirror the English one.
- Your **logo** as `.svg` or `.png` (this replaces the temporary logo I drew).
- Your **exact colours**, if you know them (e.g. `#0C3B5C`).

## Two things to know
1. **Text content stays as planned.** I will match your design's look, but the words still follow the SEO plan, and I will not put anything on the site that is not true about the business.
2. **If the design is a copy of another moving company's website**, tell me. I can match a layout style, but copying another company's exact design, text or images can cause legal trouble and looks bad to Google.

When the files are in, just say "design is ready" and I will look at it.
