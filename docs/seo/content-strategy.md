## Positioning (from research)
We have no years, review counts or awards we can truthfully claim yet, so the content earns trust by being **the clearest, most useful, most honest mover site in Dubai, in both English and Arabic**:
- It explains what drives the price instead of inventing "from AED …" figures.
- It explains permits, service lifts and timing better than competitors do.
- It states exactly what is included, and what is not.
- It makes contact instant through WhatsApp, phone and a short quote form.

## Audience questions by funnel stage
| Stage | Questions | Content that answers |
|---|---|---|
| Learn | How much does moving cost? What permits do I need? How do I prepare? | Cost guide, permit guide, checklist |
| Compare | What's included? Do they dismantle and reassemble? Do they bring materials? Can they do my area or emirate? How to choose a mover? | Service pages, area pages, route pages, "how to choose" guide, FAQ |
| Decide | Can I trust them? How fast can they come? What happens if something breaks? | About, process sections, FAQ, real reviews/projects as they arrive |
| Act | How do I book? | Get a Quote, WhatsApp, Call — on every page |

## Service page template (brief §11, adapted)
1. Hero: H1, one-line promise (no unverifiable claims), Get a Free Quote + WhatsApp
2. Service introduction: who it's for, in plain words
3. Why customers need this service (real Dubai context: permits, lifts, heat, timing)
4. What we offer / what's included (and what's not)
5. Our process (step by step; owner reviews for accuracy at the gate)
6. Types of moves (property sizes and situations)
7. Price factors (what changes the price) → link to the cost guide
8. Why choose Al Qasim Movers (only verifiable points: quotes over WhatsApp, all of Dubai + every emirate, dismantling and reassembly available, partner storage available, website in English and Arabic…; do not claim Arabic-speaking staff unless the owner confirms it)
9. Service areas (links to relevant area pages and route pages)
10. Real project images (only when real ones exist; otherwise the section is omitted)
11. Customer reviews (only real ones; otherwise omitted)
12. FAQ (5–8 questions specific to this service)
13. CTA

## Area page template (brief §12)
1. H1 "Movers in {Area}", with a short intro stating the property mix
2. Moving in {Area}: what's different (cited facts: permit authority, towers/villas, loading, access)
3. Property types we move there
4. Permits & building rules (area-specific) → link to the permit guide
5. Moving process for {Area}
6. Relevant services (3–5 links)
7. Nearby areas we also cover (links)
8. Real local projects (only if they exist)
9. Unique FAQs (3–5)
10. CTA

## Route page template (inter-emirate)
Distance and typical timing (cited), permits at both ends, utilities switchover (e.g. DEWA → ADDC), what's included, how pricing differs from local moves, FAQ, CTA.

## Blog strategy
- Launch with **5 strategic guides** (see calendar). No bulk AI articles.
- Each guide targets an informational cluster and links to 2–4 commercial pages.
- Arabic versions go first for the guides with Arabic demand (permits, cost, checklist).

## Voice & tone
- **English:** plain, confident, specific; short sentences. No hype words ("#1", "best in Dubai", "100%", "guaranteed", "zero damage"). No filler ("in today's fast-paced world").
- **Arabic:** Modern Standard Arabic that is natural and clear for Gulf readers. Use the terms people actually search (نقل اثاث، فك وتركيب، تغليف). Never keyword-stuff the way Arabic competitors do.

## Honesty rules specific to this business
- Storage: "secure storage through our partner facility". Never "our warehouse".
- Same-day moves: "may be possible depending on crew and truck availability — WhatsApp us to check". Never "guaranteed same-day".
- International: never mentioned as a service.
- Insurance, licences, years, reviews: not mentioned until verified.
- Prices: market ranges only inside the cost guide, clearly labelled as market data with sources.

## Review & approval process
Brief (seo-strategist) → outline (content-strategist) → draft EN (content-writer) → Arabic version written for Arabic search (content-writer) → integrity + duplication check (content-strategist) → owner review at the stage gate → final-reviewer.
