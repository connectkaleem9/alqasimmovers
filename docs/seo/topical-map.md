## Topical authority logic
- **Pillar = service pages.** Each owns one commercial cluster.
- **Guides** answer the informational questions around the pillars (cost, permits, checklists) and link up to them.
- **Area pages** tie services to places with real local differences (permit authority, tower vs villa, access).
- **Entities covered:** Dubai, the emirates, master developers and community managers (Emaar, Nakheel, DMCC, Dubai Properties), Ejari, DEWA/ADDC, service lifts, move permits.

## Out of scope (never covered)
- International moving (not offered)
- Cleaning, painting, pest control (not offered; partner-type services)
