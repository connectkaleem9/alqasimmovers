## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** Villa and townhouse residents with large volumes and community access rules.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Volume, gated-community access and outdoor items make this a different job from an apartment move.
3. **Unique information it provides:** Crew sizing, garden and outdoor handling, gated-community and Palm access, what raises and lowers cost.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
