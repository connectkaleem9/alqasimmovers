## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** People who want packing done properly, either standalone or with a move.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Packing is searched as its own service; unpacking merged here per the SERP evidence in the keyword map.
3. **Unique information it provides:** Material and method per item type, partial packing, unpacking options, and self-packing guidance.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
