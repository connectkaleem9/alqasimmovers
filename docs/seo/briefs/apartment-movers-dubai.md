## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** Tower residents whose move is shaped by building rules rather than volume.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Tower moves have a distinct problem set (permit, lift slot, loading bay) that the home page cannot cover without bloating.
3. **Unique information it provides:** How permits and lift slots decide the day, per-size timings, and low-rise buildings without service lifts.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
