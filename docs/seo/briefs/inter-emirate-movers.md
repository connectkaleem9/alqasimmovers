## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** People moving out of or into Dubai from another emirate.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Hub for every emirate; the two busiest routes get their own pages.
3. **Unique information it provides:** What changes over distance, the route list, and timing and traffic planning.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
