## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** Households and offices moving on the Dubai–Sharjah corridor.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** The highest-volume cross-emirate route; its constraints (traffic, stairs, parking) differ from Abu Dhabi.
3. **Unique information it provides:** Traffic-based scheduling, Sharjah access realities, and both directions.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
