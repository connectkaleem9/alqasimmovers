## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** Shops, restaurants and warehouses with stock, fixtures and trading-hour constraints.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Separated from office moves: different items, different landlords, different hours.
3. **Unique information it provides:** Stock and fixture handling, mall access rules, overnight and phased moves, the technician boundary.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
