## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** People whose dates do not line up and who need furniture held between homes.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Move-plus-store is a distinct need and a distinct search; the page also states the partner-facility fact honestly.
3. **Unique information it provides:** How move-and-store works, what not to store, Dubai summer considerations, how storage is priced.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
