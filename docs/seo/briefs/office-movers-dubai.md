## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** Businesses that must relocate without losing working days.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Commercial intent, a different buyer (office manager) and different constraints from residential moves.
3. **Unique information it provides:** Out-of-hours moving, workstation dismantling, the IT boundary, floor-plan labelling, phased moves.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
