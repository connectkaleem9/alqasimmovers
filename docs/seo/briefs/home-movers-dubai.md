## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** Someone moving a whole household inside Dubai who wants packing, dismantling and reassembly, not just a truck.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Household moves are the core service and the broadest commercial cluster; the homepage targets the brand / "movers and packers" term instead.
3. **Unique information it provides:** Permit and service-lift reality, what the standard move includes, and what actually changes the price.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
