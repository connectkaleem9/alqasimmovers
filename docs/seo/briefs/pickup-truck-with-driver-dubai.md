## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** People who need transport (optionally with labour) rather than a full moving service.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Transactional intent at a different price point; it also absorbs the loading/unloading cluster.
3. **Unique information it provides:** What it suits, vehicle sizing, the labour add-on, and an honest comparison with a full move.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
