# SEO Brief — About

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| URL | `/about/` |
| Page type | core |
| Status | Published 2026-09-22 |

## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** People checking who the company is before trusting it with their belongings.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Trust page: who we are, how we work, what we do and do not do. Only verified facts.
3. **Unique information it provides:** see the H2 outline below.
4. **Action the user should take:** request a quote, call, or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the related-links block at the foot of the page.

## Targeting
- **Primary (EN):** Al Qasim Movers
- **Primary (AR):** القاسم لنقل الأثاث

## On-page
- **H1:** About Al Qasim Movers
- **Meta title (53 chars):** About Al Qasim Movers | Movers &amp; Packers in Dubai
- **Meta description (145 chars):** Al Qasim Movers L.L.C is a Dubai moving company: packing, dismantling, moving and reassembly across all of Dubai and to every emirate in the UAE.
- **H2s:** How we work; What we move; Where we work; Honest about what we publish
- **Indexable:** yes

## Integrity notes
No invented facts, no price list, no guarantees that are not verified in `config/business.json`.
