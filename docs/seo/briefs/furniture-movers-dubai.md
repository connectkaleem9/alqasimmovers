## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** People moving a few pieces or one item, including marketplace purchases.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** Small-job intent with a very different price expectation from a full move.
3. **Unique information it provides:** What single-item jobs include, why a crew matters for one item, and when a truck-only booking is cheaper.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
