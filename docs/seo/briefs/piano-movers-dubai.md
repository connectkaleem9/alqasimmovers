## Five-question test
1. **Search intent satisfied:** Owners of heavy or awkward items who need specialist handling.
2. **Why this page deserves to exist:** High-anxiety, low-volume searches that convert; the answer is access-based and cannot be a line on another page.
3. **Unique information it provides:** Which heavy items we take, why access decides the price, and an honest limit on grand pianos and specialist items.
4. **Action the user should take:** send a list, photos or a video and get a price, by phone or WhatsApp.
5. **Internal pages it connects to:** the "Related services" block at the foot of the page, plus contextual links in the body (see `docs/seo/internal-link-map.md`).
