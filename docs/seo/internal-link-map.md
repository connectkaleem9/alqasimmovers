## Global links (header/footer, every page)
- **Header:** Services (menu), Areas, Guides, About, Contact, **Get a Free Quote** button, phone, language switch (links to the same page in the other language)
- **Footer:** all service pages, all area pages, guides hub, FAQ, legal pages, NAP
- **Mobile sticky bar:** Call Now | WhatsApp

Header and footer links give every page a click depth of 1–2. Contextual links (below) carry the relevance.

## Rules
- Descriptive, varied anchors. No repeated exact-match anchors in body copy.
- No orphans; click depth ≤ 3 (expected ≤ 2 through header/footer).
- Arabic pages link only to Arabic pages (plus the language switcher).
- Links point to the canonical trailing-slash URL.
