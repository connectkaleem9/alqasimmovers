## Strategy in five moves (evidence in business-research and competitor-analysis)
1. **Don't fight marketplaces for the head term first.** The homepage targets it, but growth comes from specific service, route and area pages.
2. **Bilingual advantage.** A native-quality Arabic mirror with correct hreflang. The Arabic SERP is weak (stuffed, single-language sites).
3. **Local usefulness.** Permit and building-rule content and cited area differences beat community-name lists.
4. **Honest transparency.** Price factors, clear inclusions, conditional same-day wording. This builds trust without fake signals.
5. **Technical edge.** Static, lightweight pages (competitors run 24–122 scripts and 160 KB–1.8 MB HTML) with clean, truthful schema.

## Page types & roles
Home (brand + core) · service pillars (commercial) · route pages (inter-emirate commercial) · area pages (local) · guides (informational support) · FAQ (objections) · quote/contact (conversion) · projects/reviews (proof; noindex until real items exist).

## On-page, local and content standards
`.claude/rules/seo-rules.md`, `local-seo-strategy.md`, `content-strategy.md`, `.claude/rules/bilingual-rules.md`.

## Link acquisition (legitimate only)
Directory citations (UAE), marketplace listings, and the permit guide as a naturally linkable resource. Post-launch: outreach to expat and community content. No paid or spam links.

## Measurement plan
Search Console (domain property, both languages) plus consent-aware Analytics events: `quote_submit`, `tel_click`, `whatsapp_click`, each with a `lang` parameter. Monthly review adds new topics and pages, and decides deferred area pages.

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| No GBP means no Maps/Local Pack | Organic focus; recommend creating a GBP later |
| No reviews or projects at launch | Pages noindexed until real; trust through clarity |
| Head term dominated by marketplaces | Long-tail and bilingual focus; consider marketplace listings as a lead channel |
| Arabic quality | Native-style writing + owner review at the gate |
| Permit rules change | The permit guide shows a "last checked" date and is reviewed quarterly |
