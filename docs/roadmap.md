## First release target (brief §44)
Bilingual: each page exists in English and Arabic (≈ 25–40 unique pages × 2 languages). Approximately 25–40 genuinely useful pages: Home, About, Services hub, 12–15 validated service pages, 8–12 validated area pages, Projects, Reviews, FAQ, Get a Quote, Contact, Blog (+ a few strategic posts), legal pages, 404. Expand later based on Search Console data and real business coverage.
