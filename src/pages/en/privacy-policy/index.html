Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we never do with it.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy covers alqasimmovers.com and the information Al Qasim Movers L.L.C receives through it. If anything here is unclear, email {{business.email}}.

What we collect

When you request a quote: your name, phone number, and optionally your email address, the addresses you are moving from and to, the property type, your moving date, the services you need and any message you write. We also store a one-way encoded form of your IP address to stop spam, which cannot be turned back into your address.

When you submit a review: your name, your rating and your review text, optionally your email address, area and service. Your email is never shown publicly; only the review, the name and the area appear on the site.

If you accept cookies: Google Analytics data about pages visited and buttons tapped. Nothing is loaded until you press Accept — details are in the Cookie Policy.

We do not ask for, and you should never send us, passport copies, Emirates ID numbers, bank details or card numbers through this website.

Why we use it

To prepare your quote, to contact you about your move, and to plan the crew and vehicle. Review details are used to publish your review and, if needed, to check that a review came from a real customer.

We do not sell your information, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not send marketing messages to people who only asked for a price.

Where it is kept

Quote requests and reviews are stored in a database on our hosting in a location that is not reachable from the public website, and it is accessible only through a password-protected admin area used by the business owner.

Quote details are also emailed to the business so the team can respond quickly.

How long we keep it

Quote requests are kept while they are useful for serving customers and for our own records. Published reviews stay on the site until you ask us to remove them.

You can ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing {{business.email}} from the address you used, or by messaging us on WhatsApp from the number you gave us. Tell us what to remove and we will confirm when it is done.

Your choices

You can use the site without accepting cookies; analytics simply will not load. You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

This website links to other sites, such as WhatsApp and Google. Once you follow those links, their own privacy terms apply.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: {{business.email}} or {{business.phone.local}}.
