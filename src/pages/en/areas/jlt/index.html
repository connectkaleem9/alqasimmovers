Movers in JLT (Jumeirah Lakes Towers)

Towers in clusters, a free-zone authority above the building managements, and offices and apartments in the same buildings.

JLT is laid out in clusters, each with its own towers, its own parking and its own basement access. Above the individual building managements sits DMCC as the free-zone authority, so moves here often involve one process for the tower and another for the authority, particularly for offices.

We move apartments and offices across every cluster, including moves between JLT and Dubai Marina, which are close enough to feel simple and still need two permits and two lift bookings.

Local detail

What a JLT move involves

  • Cluster layout

    Getting a truck to the right tower entrance matters here; the cluster and tower letter save an hour of driving in circles.

  • DMCC and building rules

    Building management handles the lift and permit; offices may also have free-zone requirements for access and contractors.

  • Basement parking limits

    Height restrictions in some basements decide the vehicle size, so we confirm before the day rather than on arrival.

  • Mixed-use towers

    Offices and apartments share lifts, so moving hours are often restricted to evenings and weekends.

  • Two-permit moves

    JLT to Marina, or cluster to cluster, means a permit and a slot at both ends.

  • Lift protection

    Standard in JLT towers, and usually a condition of the permit.

Apartments and offices

JLT is one of the few communities where a mover needs to be equally comfortable with a family apartment and a 20-desk office in the same week. For business moves, see office movers — the plan is built around evening or weekend access, which JLT towers usually require anyway.

For home moves, apartment moving covers the tower side, and packing is the most common addition.

Moving in JLT

Do I need a permit from DMCC or from my building?

For a normal apartment move, the permit comes from your building management. Office moves and contractor work can involve the free-zone process as well, so check with your building first.

Can you move an office in JLT at the weekend?

Yes, and most JLT towers prefer it. Lifts are shared with residents, so out-of-hours moves cause fewer conflicts.

Is a JLT to Marina move simple?

Physically yes, administratively no. It is a short drive and two separate permits and lift slots.

Will your truck fit the basement?

Tell us the tower and we will check the height restriction, then send a vehicle that fits or plan a ground-level loading point.
