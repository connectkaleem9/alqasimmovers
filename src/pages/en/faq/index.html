Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we are asked before almost every move, answered plainly.

Booking and prices

How much does moving cost in Dubai?

It depends on volume, the floor and access at both ends, and how much packing you want. That is why we ask for a list or a video instead of publishing a price list. Quotes are free — send your details.

Do you charge for the quote or a site visit?

No. Quotes are free and most are done from photos or a video.

How far in advance should I book?

A few days is usually enough. The last weekend of the month is the busiest time in Dubai, so book earlier if you are moving then.

Can you move on the same day?

Sometimes, depending on crew and truck availability. Call and we will tell you honestly whether we can — we never promise a slot we cannot keep.

How do I pay?

Payment terms are confirmed in writing with your quote before you book, so there are no surprises on the day.

Permits and buildings

Do I need a permit to move?

Most managed buildings in Dubai require a move-in or move-out permit from building management, and security will not let a crew upstairs without it. Villas in gated communities usually need the truck registered at the gate instead.

Who applies for the permit, you or me?

The permit is issued to the resident, so you apply. We give you whatever crew and vehicle details the building asks for.

What if my building has no service lift?

We send a bigger crew. Older buildings in areas like Deira and Bur Dubai often have stairs only, and that is a crew-size question, not a problem.

Can you move in the evening or at weekends?

Yes. Many towers only allow moves in set hours, and evening or weekend slots are common.

On the day

Do you bring boxes and packing material?

Yes — boxes, bubble wrap, stretch film, tape and blankets come with the service.

Will you dismantle and reassemble my furniture?

Yes. Beds, wardrobes, tables and desks are taken apart and rebuilt, with fittings bagged and labelled.

How long does a move take?

A studio or one-bedroom flat is usually a half day, a two or three-bedroom home most of a day, and a large villa with full packing is often split over two days.

Do you take the packing material away?

Yes, we remove the boxes and material we used.

What will you not move?

Anything hazardous or perishable — gas cylinders, flammable liquids, chemicals, plants that will not survive, and open food. Cash, jewellery, passports and documents should travel with you, not in the truck.

Coverage and extras

Which areas do you cover?

All of Dubai. See the areas we serve for the communities with their own rules.

Do you move to other emirates?

Yes, to every emirate in the UAE. See moving between emirates. We do not do international moves.

Can you store my furniture between homes?

Yes, through a partner storage facility. We pack it, move it in, and deliver and rebuild it when you are ready — see storage services.

Can you hang curtains or mount a TV after the move?

Yes, curtain and blind installation, TV wall mounting and fixing wall-mounted furniture — see assembly and handyman work.

Something was damaged. What now?

Tell us immediately, on the day if possible, and send photos. We will look at what happened and come back to you.
