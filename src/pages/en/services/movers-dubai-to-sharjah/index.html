Movers from Dubai to Sharjah

A short move on the map and a long one in traffic. We plan the day around the peak, not through it.

Dubai to Sharjah is the country's busiest moving corridor, and also its most underestimated. The distance is small, so people assume it is a quick job — then the truck sits on Ittihad Road at five in the afternoon with a loaded wardrobe and a family waiting.

We move households and offices from every part of Dubai to Al Nahda, Al Majaz, Al Qasimia, Muwailih, Al Khan, Abu Shagara and the rest of Sharjah, planning the loading and the drive around peak hours.

The plan

How we handle the route

  • Beat the peak

    Loading starts early so the drive falls outside the worst of the Dubai–Sharjah rush.

  • One trip where possible

    Even a short route costs an hour or more per extra run once traffic is counted.

  • Older buildings, no service lift

    Common in parts of Sharjah, so we send a bigger crew where there are stairs.

  • Permits at both ends

    Dubai tower rules on one side, Sharjah building management on the other.

  • Same-day reassembly

    Beds and wardrobes rebuilt so the family sleeps normally that night.

  • Both directions

    Sharjah to Dubai moves are just as common, and work the same way.

Parking and access in Sharjah

The practical issues in Sharjah are usually parking and stairs rather than permits. Many residential streets in Al Nahda and Al Majaz are tight, with limited space for a truck near the entrance, and older buildings often have no service lift.

Both are manageable when we know in advance: a bigger crew for stairs, and a vehicle that fits the street. Send a photo of the building entrance if you are unsure — it takes a second and it changes the plan.

What the move costs

Volume, floors and access decide it, with the traffic factored into the time rather than hidden as a surprise. A second-floor flat with no lift takes longer than a fifth-floor flat with one, which is not the order people expect.

Dubai to Sharjah questions

How early do you start?

Early. The Dubai–Sharjah road decides the day, so loading usually begins in the morning to keep the drive outside peak hours.

My Sharjah building has no lift. Is that a problem?

No, but it changes the crew size. Tell us the floor and we will plan for stairs rather than discover them.

Do you move from Sharjah to Dubai as well?

Yes, that direction is just as common.

Can you move on a Friday or weekend?

Yes, and weekends can be easier for access, though buildings sometimes restrict the hours.

Do you move offices to Sharjah?

Yes, including industrial and free-zone areas.
