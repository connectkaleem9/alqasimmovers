Moving Between Emirates

Dubai to anywhere in the UAE, in one trip where possible, with the same packing, dismantling and reassembly as a local move.

People move out of Dubai for space and rent, and into Dubai for work. Either way, an inter-emirate move is mostly a timing problem: one long drive, two sets of building rules, and a single day in which everything has to be unloaded and rebuilt.

We move households and offices from Dubai to every emirate — Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain and Al Ain — and back again. We do not do international moves.

What changes

How an inter-emirate move differs

  • One trip, properly sized

    The truck is sized so everything travels at once. A second trip to Abu Dhabi costs a day, not an hour.

  • Early start

    Long-distance moves start early so unloading and reassembly finish the same day.

  • Two sets of rules

    Permits, lift slots and security at both ends, in two different emirates.

  • Packed for the distance

    More road time means tighter wrapping and better stacking than a cross-town move.

  • Reassembly included

    Beds and wardrobes rebuilt on arrival, not left in pieces because the crew has a long drive home.

  • Offices too

    Business moves out of Dubai to free zones and industrial areas in other emirates.

Timing and traffic

Timing is the difference between a smooth move and a long one. The Dubai–Sharjah corridor is heavy at peak hours in both directions, and the Abu Dhabi road is fast but long enough that a late start eats the afternoon.

Inter-emirate questions

Which emirates do you move to?

All of them: Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain and Al Ain, plus moves within Dubai.

Can the move be done in one day?

For most homes, yes, with an early start. Large villas with full packing are usually split across two days.

Do you move from another emirate back to Dubai?

Yes, the route works both ways.

Do you handle international moves?

No. We move within the UAE only.

Is it more expensive than moving inside Dubai?

The distance adds driving time and fuel, so yes, but the packing and the crew work the same way. You get one price covering the whole job.
