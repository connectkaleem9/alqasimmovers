Furniture Storage in Dubai

When the new place is not ready, we move your furniture into partner storage and bring it back when you are.

Moves rarely line up neatly. A tenancy ends before the next one starts, a handover slips by a few weeks, or a villa is being renovated and the furniture has to go somewhere in the meantime.

We handle the whole in-between: packing, dismantling, moving your furniture into storage at our partner facility, and then delivering and reassembling it at the new address when you are ready. You deal with one company for both ends instead of hiring movers twice and a storage company in between.

Storage is provided through a partner facility, not our own warehouse — we say that plainly so you know exactly who is holding your furniture.

What to store and what not to

Furniture, appliances, boxes, and anything you will not need for a while store well. What does not: food, liquids, aerosols and anything perishable, and documents or valuables you might need at short notice, which are better kept with you.

Dubai's summer is the reason to ask one question before storing anything sensitive: candles, some electronics and certain wooden items are happier in temperature-controlled space. Tell us what you are storing and we will tell you what the facility can and cannot offer, rather than guessing on your behalf.

What storage costs

Storage is priced by the space your items actually take and how long they stay, so dismantling wardrobes and beds genuinely reduces the bill. Moving in and moving out are separate pieces of work from the storage itself, and we quote them together so there are no surprises.

Storage questions

Is the warehouse yours?

No. We use a partner storage facility and we are upfront about that. We handle the packing, the move in and the delivery back out.

How long can my furniture stay?

From a few weeks to longer-term. Tell us the expected dates and we will confirm what the facility can do.

Can I take some items out before the rest?

Tell us at the start if you expect to, because it changes how we pack and stack the load.

Do you pack for storage as well?

Yes. Packing for storage is part of the service, and it is not the same as packing for a one-hour drive across town.

Will my furniture be reassembled on delivery?

Yes — delivered, carried in and rebuilt, the same as a normal move.
