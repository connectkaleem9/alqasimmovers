Permits, lifts and timing

Most Dubai buildings ask for a move-in or move-out permit from building management before they let a moving crew use the service lift. The process varies by developer and management company — Emaar, Nakheel, DMCC and the big property managers each have their own form, and some want a security deposit or an NOC from the landlord first.

Apply as soon as your dates are fixed, because the permit and the lift slot are the two things most likely to delay a move. Once you have the slot, tell us the time window and we plan the crew around it. If your move is in a tower, the apartment moving page explains the tower-specific parts in more detail.

Villas are a different job: more volume, garden items and often stairs instead of a lift. That is covered on the villa movers page.

What affects the price of a house move

Volume first: a studio is a couple of hours with a small crew, while a four-bedroom home is a full day. Then the floor and the access — a ground-floor move with a loading bay next to the door is quicker than a sixth-floor move with a shared lift and a long walk from the parking.

After that it is the extras: how much packing you want us to do, whether beds and wardrobes need dismantling, heavy or awkward pieces, and whether the new place is ready or the furniture needs to go into storage for a while.

House moving questions

Can you move my home in one day?

Most homes up to three bedrooms are a single-day move, including reassembly. Bigger homes with a lot of packing sometimes run into a second day, and we tell you that before you book, not on the day.

Do you pack for me, or should I pack myself?

Either. Many people pack their own clothes and books and leave the kitchen, glassware and electronics to us. Full packing is the most common choice for family homes.

Can you do a same-day move?

Sometimes, depending on crew and truck availability that day. Call or WhatsApp and we will tell you honestly whether we can.

Do you move to other emirates?

Yes. We move households from Dubai to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain and Al Ain.

What do you need from me before moving day?

The two addresses with floor numbers, your permit and lift slot if the building needs one, parking access, and a note of anything fragile, very heavy, or not to be moved.
