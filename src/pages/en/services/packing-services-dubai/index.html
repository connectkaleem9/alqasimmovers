Packing & Unpacking Services in Dubai

Professional packing with proper materials, whether you want the whole home done or just the kitchen and the fragile things.

Most breakages happen because of how something was packed, not how it was carried. Plates packed flat instead of on edge, a television in a box without corner protection, a half-empty box crushed under a full one — these are the things that cost people their crockery and their screens.

Our packing service in Dubai uses the right material for each item: double-walled boxes for books, bubble wrap and dish cells for crockery and glass, blankets and stretch film for furniture, and wardrobe boxes so clothes travel on their hangers. Everything is labelled by room, so unpacking is not a treasure hunt.

You can book packing on its own, or as part of a home move, villa move or office relocation.

Materials and method

How we pack

  • Kitchen and glassware

    Plates on edge in dish cells, glasses individually wrapped, and the box marked fragile and this-way-up.

  • Electronics

    Screens, PCs and consoles wrapped and boxed with padding on the corners; cables bagged and labelled per device.

  • Clothes

    Wardrobe boxes so hanging clothes stay on their hangers and arrive wearable.

  • Artwork and mirrors

    Corner protection and rigid packing, carried and loaded on edge, never flat.

  • Furniture

    Blankets, then stretch film. Sofas and mattresses get covers so nothing rubs in the truck.

  • Labelling

    Every box marked with its room and contents, so the crew unloads it where it belongs.

Unpacking, when you want it

Unpacking is the part people underestimate. We can unpack everything and take the cardboard away the same day, or just do the kitchen and bedrooms so you can sleep and cook while you deal with the rest at your own pace.

If you prefer to unpack yourself, we still take the packing material away later rather than leaving it stacked in your hallway.

Packing on your own? Do these four things

Start with what you do not use: books, out-of-season clothes, spare linen. Leave the kitchen and bathroom until last. Second, keep boxes under a weight you can lift with one hand under the base — books in small boxes, linen in big ones.

Third, tape the bottom of every box across the seam, not just along it. Fourth, write the room on the side of the box rather than the top, because you will be reading them in a stack.

Packing questions

Do you bring the boxes and materials?

Yes. Boxes, bubble wrap, stretch film, tape and blankets come with the service.

Can you pack only part of the house?

Yes, and it is common. Most people ask for the kitchen, glassware and electronics and pack their own clothes and books.

How long does packing a home take?

A one-bedroom apartment is a few hours. A family villa with a full kitchen usually takes a day, which is why packing is often done the day before the move.

Do you unpack as well?

Yes, fully or partly, and we remove the packing material afterwards.

Can you pack the day before moving day?

Yes. For larger homes we normally recommend it, so moving day is only loading, driving and reassembly.
