Movers from Dubai to Abu Dhabi

An early start, one truck, and your beds rebuilt in Abu Dhabi the same evening.

Dubai to Abu Dhabi is around 140 kilometres, which is a comfortable drive and a demanding moving day. Loading, driving, unloading and reassembly have to fit into one stretch, so the time you start decides whether the job finishes in daylight.

We move homes and offices from anywhere in Dubai to Abu Dhabi city, Khalifa City, Al Reem Island, Yas Island, Saadiyat, Mussafah and the surrounding communities, with the same packing, dismantling and reassembly as a local move.

What to arrange before moving day

Confirm the access at the Abu Dhabi end first. Many Abu Dhabi towers want a move permit and a lift booking exactly as Dubai does, and some communities register the vehicle and crew at the gate. Doing that a few days earlier removes most of the risk from the day.

Second, plan your utilities: closing accounts in Dubai and opening them in Abu Dhabi is a different set of providers, and arriving to a flat without power makes an otherwise good move feel like a bad one.

Third, keep an overnight bag with you rather than in the truck. On long moves, the last box is not always unpacked by bedtime.

Pricing a Dubai to Abu Dhabi move

The price comes from the volume, the access at both ends, and how much packing you want, plus the distance. The distance part is predictable; the access part is what varies, so tell us the floor and lift situation at both ends when you ask.
