Truck with driver, or a full move?

The honest answer is that transport alone works when the item is simple, the access is easy and you have help. Once you are lifting furniture down stairs, taking a wardrobe apart or protecting a lift lobby, a crew that does this daily is faster and cheaper than a cheap vehicle plus an afternoon of damage.

Truck and driver questions

Can I get a truck without workers?

Yes. Vehicle and driver only is fine if you have your own help at both ends.

How quickly can a truck come?

Often the same or next day, depending on availability. Call or WhatsApp with the two addresses and what you are moving.

What size truck will come?

The size is chosen from your load. Send a photo or a list and we will send the right vehicle, not the one we want to fill.

Do you help carry things up to the apartment?

Yes, when you book loading crew with the truck. Tell us the floor and whether there is a lift.

Do you cover deliveries to other emirates?

Yes, journeys to any emirate are possible. See moves between emirates.
