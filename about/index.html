About Al Qasim Movers

A Dubai movers and packers company that does the whole job — packing, dismantling, moving and putting it all back together.

Al Qasim Movers L.L.C is a moving and packing company based in Dubai. We move homes, apartments, villas and offices across every part of the city, and we move households and businesses to every emirate in the UAE.

What we do is simple to describe and hard to do well: arrive when we said we would, pack things so they survive the trip, take furniture apart without damaging it or the building, load in an order that makes sense, and rebuild everything at the other end so the day actually finishes.

We have completed more than 1,200 moves and hold a 4.9 average customer rating.

Our approach

How we work

  • We ask before we price

    Volume, floors, lifts and access decide the crew and the truck. We ask about them so the price is real rather than a guess that changes on the day.

  • The crew brings everything

    Tools, boxes, bubble wrap, stretch film, blankets and straps. You should not have to buy packing material at the last minute.

  • We protect the building too

    Lift and floor protection where management requires it — that is what keeps your deposit as well as your furniture.

  • We reassemble

    A move is not finished when the truck is empty. Beds and wardrobes go back together the same day.

  • We tell you what we cannot do

    If a job needs a specialist, or a same-day slot is not available, we say so instead of promising and improvising.

  • Two languages

    We work in English and Arabic, and the whole site is available in both.

What we move

Households of every size, from a studio to a five-bedroom villa, plus offices, shops and warehouses. Alongside full moves we do packing and unpacking, dismantling and assembly, single-item moves, heavy items such as pianos and safes, and storage through a partner facility when dates do not line up.

We do not do international moving. If you are shipping a household outside the UAE, you need a freight forwarder, and we will tell you that rather than take the booking.

Where we work

All of Dubai: the tower communities like Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay and Downtown; the villa communities like Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills and Palm Jumeirah; and everywhere in between, including Deira, Bur Dubai, Al Quoz and Mirdif.

Outside Dubai we move to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain and Al Ain — see moving between emirates.

Honest about what we publish

Everything on this website is either something we do or something anyone can verify. There are no invented reviews, no stock photos pretending to be our jobs, and no price list that changes once we see the real volume.

Reviews come from customers through the reviews page, and the projects gallery shows photos from moves we have actually done.
