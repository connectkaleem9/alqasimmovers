# Conversion Optimization

Project: Al Qasim Movers — movers & packers, Dubai (https://alqasimmovers.com). Follow `CLAUDE.md` and the rules in `.claude/rules/`, especially `content-integrity-rules.md`.

## Instructions
1. Every important page: primary 'Get a Free Quote', secondary 'WhatsApp Us', mobile sticky 'Call Now | WhatsApp'.
2. Above the fold on mobile: what we do, where, and a way to contact.
3. Quote form: minimum required fields (name, phone, moving from/to, date); others optional; clear labels; inline validation; success page with next steps.
4. Trust: real reviews, real photos, confirmed facts only; address common objections (pricing transparency, damage handling, timing) with owner-approved answers.
5. Track: form submit, tel: click, WhatsApp click (analytics events, consent-aware).

## Checklist
- [ ] CTAs present on page
- [ ] Sticky bar on mobile without covering content
- [ ] Form ≤ necessary fields
- [ ] Only real trust signals
- [ ] Events defined

## Expected Output
Conversion notes per template + `docs/seo/conversion-plan.md`.
