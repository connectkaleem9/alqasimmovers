# Competitor Research

Project: Al Qasim Movers — movers & packers, Dubai (https://alqasimmovers.com). Follow `CLAUDE.md` and the rules in `.claude/rules/`, especially `content-integrity-rules.md`.

## Instructions
1. Pick 8–12 competitors ranking for core terms (movers dubai, movers and packers dubai, villa movers dubai, office movers dubai).
2. For each: URL structure, service & area pages, title/meta/H1/H2 samples, content depth, CTAs, reviews, trust signals, images (stock vs real), FAQs, schema (view source / Rich Results Test), PageSpeed mobile score, mobile UX notes.
3. Identify patterns and gaps: questions nobody answers well, thin area pages, missing process detail, poor mobile UX.
4. Note legitimate backlink/citation sources competitors appear on (directories, local listings).

## Checklist
- [ ] 8–12 competitors with discovery method
- [ ] All analysis fields filled per competitor
- [ ] Cross-competitor pattern summary
- [ ] Gap list with opportunities
- [ ] No copied text

## Expected Output
`docs/research/competitor-analysis.md` with a per-competitor table and an 'Opportunities for Al Qasim' section.
